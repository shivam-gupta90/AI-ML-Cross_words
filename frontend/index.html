📖 How To Play & Rules

1. Crossword Navigation

Click any active cell on the 15×15 grid or any clue in the list to select a word. Clicking an intersection cell toggles between Across and Down.

2. Unified Answer Submission

You do not type letters individually into cells. Type the entire word or phrase into the single answer box below the clue and hit Submit or press Enter.

3. Crossing Words & Hidden Letters

Solving one word reveals letters that intersect with other words across the grid. Use intersections to deduce difficult AI/ML terms!

4. Limited Attempts & Countdown

You have 3 attempts. Each wrong submission deducts an attempt and reduces score by 100 points. The 10-minute timer runs continuously.

5. Hint System & Scoring

Base Score: +100 per word solved • Victory Bonus: +500 • Time Bonus: +2 × Remaining Seconds • Hint Penalty: -50 pts per hint used.
